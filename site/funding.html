Prepare an application

  • Check the qualification, study mode, residence or nationality rules, and whether an offer or enrolment is required.
  • List your tuition fees, living costs and project expenses, alongside confirmed support and the remaining shortfall.
  • Explain how the course or project meets the funder’s aims. Prepare any references and evidence requested.
  • Check payment dates, whether awards can be combined and any reporting conditions before relying on the funding.

For advice about your course and university-administered awards, use the contact details on your postgraduate course page.
