What do we look for?

We prioritise insect science, crop protection and biological recording, including species identification, habitat surveys, ecological monitoring and biodiversity data. Recording opportunities can cover plants, fungi and other wildlife as well as insects. Broader roles need a clear field, identification or analytical connection. Each advert explains its value; course interest indicates a skills connection, not guaranteed eligibility. Generic sustainability administration and unrelated uses of “IPM” are excluded. “MRes” means an advertised research opportunity, not a generic taught course.
